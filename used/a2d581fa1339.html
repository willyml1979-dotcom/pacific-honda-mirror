At its core, the Defender 110 P300 S is powered by a potent 2.0 L Ingenium turbocharged inline‑4 engine, producing 296 hp and 295 lb‑ft of torque. Paired with a seamless 8‑speed automatic transmission and permanent all‑wheel drive with electronic transfer case, this model strikes a balance between confident on‑road performance and true off‑road capability. Properly engineered for Canadian conditions, it offers the ability to wade through up to 900 mm (35.4″) of water and tow up to approximately 3,629 kg (8,000 lb) with a trailer-equipped hitch, and delivers estimated fuel efficiency of around 13.2 L/100 km city and 11.9 L/100 km highway.

The cabin of the P300 S is thoughtfully designed for both comfort and connectivity. A standard 11.4″ Pivi Pro touchscreen infotainment system includes integrated navigation, SiriusXM HD radio, Bluetooth audio, wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, and wireless device charging. A Meridian™ premium sound system heightens the audio experience, while dual‑zone automatic climate control—complete with under‑seat ducts—ensures interior comfort across seasons. Additional refinement comes via standard keyless entry with push-button start, remote engine start, HomeLink garage-door opener, and a heated leather-wrapped steering wheel.

Safety is comprehensive in this model, with a full suite of advanced driver-assistance systems included as standard. That lineup features adaptive cruise control with stop‑and‑go functionality, lane‑keeping assist, blind‑spot assist, forward-collision warning with autonomous emergency braking, rear‑traffic monitor, and a 360° surround-view camera system. LED projector headlights with auto high‑beam assist, fog lamps, rain‑sensing wipers, heated power-folding and auto-dimming side mirrors, perimeter and approach lighting, and deep‑tinted glass enhance both premium presence and confidence on every journey.

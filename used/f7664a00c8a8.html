The 2025 Land Rover Defender 110 P300 S is a testament to Land Rover's commitment to blending rugged capability with refined luxury. Powered by a 2.0-litre turbocharged four-cylinder engine, it delivers a harmonious balance of performance and efficiency, ensuring confident driving across diverse terrains. The Defender 110 P300 S comes equipped with all-wheel drive and an eight-speed automatic transmission, providing seamless power delivery and exceptional traction. Its Terrain Response 2 system and electronic air suspension adapt to varying conditions, enhancing both comfort and control.

Inside, the Defender 110 P300 S offers a suite of standard features designed to elevate the driving experience. The Pivi Pro infotainment system, displayed on a 13.1-inch touchscreen, integrates seamlessly with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, ensuring connectivity on the go. A Meridian sound system fills the cabin with rich audio, while wireless device charging keeps your electronics powered. Convenience is paramount, with features like keyless entry, a powered tailgate, and a 12V power socket in the cargo area. The cabin also boasts dual-zone climate control, a heated steering wheel, and a rearview mirror with auto-dimming capabilities, all contributing to a comfortable and user-friendly environment.

Safety and driver assistance are at the forefront of the Defender 110 P300 S's design. Standard features include adaptive cruise control, lane keep assist, blind spot assist, and rear traffic monitor, all working in tandem to enhance driver confidence. The 3D surround camera system provides comprehensive visibility, aiding in parking and maneuvering. Additional safety features encompass emergency braking, traffic sign recognition with adaptive speed limiter, and a suite of stability and traction controls, ensuring peace of mind on every journey.

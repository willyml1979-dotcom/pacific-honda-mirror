Experience thrilling AMG performance and open-air luxury in this Mercedes-AMG C 43 4MATIC Cabriolet. Premium Package: Burmester surround sound system, 64-colour ambient lighting, illuminated door sills, and premium comfort features.

Intelligent Drive Package: Advanced driver assistance features, including adaptive cruise control, active steering assist, and lane-keeping assistance.

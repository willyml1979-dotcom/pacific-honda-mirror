Combining extreme supercar performance with everyday SUV luxury, this 2023 Audi RS Q8 4.0T quattro 8sp Tiptronic is powered by a relentless 4.0L twin-turbocharged V8 engine producing 591 horsepower and 590 lb-ft of torque, delivered through an 8-speed Tiptronic automatic transmission and Audi’s legendary permanent quattro all-wheel drive. Highlighting its muscular, broad-shouldered stance, this flagship high-performance crossover features the 23-inch Wheel Package with bold alloy wheels in a 5-Y-rotor design, eye-catching red brake calipers, and a factory-installed trailer hitch with up to 7,700 lbs of towing capacity. Dynamic capability reaches the pinnacle of precision engineering with the Dynamic Package—incorporating quattro sport rear differential, electromechanical active roll stabilization, and an increased top speed limit—complemented by standard adaptive air suspension sport and all-wheel steering. Inside, the opulent driver-centric cabin surrounds you in Valcona leather RS sport seats with honeycomb stitching, a power panoramic glass sunroof, the 12.3-inch Audi Virtual Cockpit with RS-specific performance displays, top-view 360-degree camera, and the studio-quality 1,920-watt 23-speaker Bang & Olufsen Advanced 3D Sound System, offering an uncompromised fusion of track-bred power, prestige, and utility.
